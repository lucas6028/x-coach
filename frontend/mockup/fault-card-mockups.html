Fault Card — design options

Six directions for the coaching fault card, rendered in the real tray dark theme with live tokens. Every card shows the same data a detection carries: timecode, fault, phase, severity, the measured evidence, the KG cause and injury risk, and the corrective cue. Each column shows a High-severity active card (playhead inside) plus a Moderate inactive one so you can judge state and severity theming. Tell me the number to apply.

01 · Baseline (current)

Today's card, for reference. Temporal rail, boxed sections, cue in a green tint.

02 · Severity Spine

A bold severity edge carries the signal. Flat body, cue as a full-width footer. Calm, scannable.

03 · Diagnostic Readout

The measured number is the hero. Instrument-panel feel, fitting a biomechanics tool. Denser, sharper corners.

04 · Causal Ladder

Reads as a chain: cause leads to risk leads to fix. Most explanatory, best for teaching the "why".

05 · Rail Timeline

Keeps the temporal rail but goes airy: dividers instead of boxes, severity as an underline. Editorial, light.
